Good to know

A few plain answers.

What happens to the deposit?

The deposit covers work already done, so it isn’t refundable once the build or edits have started. You can walk away any time before go-live and you won’t owe the remaining balance.

Who owns the site?

Your domain is registered in your name, in your own account, and that login is yours from day one. I own the design and code until you’ve paid in full. Then you own your content and site.

Do I have to share my passwords?

No. You never share passwords or email access. Your Google, Facebook, Instagram and booking accounts stay yours, and you add me as a manager. They connect through embed codes and invites you send or approve.

Where is my site hosted?

On my systems, the same way this site is. If you ever leave, you get a complete copy of your site and help moving it.
