Good to know

A few plain answers.

What happens to the deposit?

The deposit covers work already done, so it isn’t refundable once the build or edits have started. You can walk away any time before go-live and you won’t owe the remaining balance.

Who owns the site?

Josh owns the design and code until you’ve paid in full. Then you own your content and site, and you can have the domain transferred to you anytime.

Do I have to share my passwords?

No. You never share passwords or email access. Booking, Google, and Facebook connect through embed codes and invites you send or approve.

Where is my site hosted?

Josh registers your domain on his Cloudflare account and hosts your site on his GitHub Pages and Cloudflare accounts, the same way this site is hosted.
