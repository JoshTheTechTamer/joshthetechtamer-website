Your information

Privacy in plain English.

What this website collects, why it is used, and the choices you have.

Last updated: October 12, 2026. This policy covers joshthetechtamer.com, operated by The Tech Tamer LLC in Muscatine, Iowa.

When you ask for help

The contact form asks for your name, a phone number or email address, the service you need, and a description. You may also provide your city and a good time to reach you. This information is used to respond, arrange service, and maintain records of the work you request. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details, sign-in codes, or other sensitive information through the form.

How requests reach Josh

The form uses Cloudflare to process requests and check for spam through Turnstile. Request details are emailed to Josh’s Gmail inbox through the site’s mail service. Cloudflare and Google process information as service providers. Technical information such as an IP address and browser details may be processed to deliver and protect the site.

Optional website analytics

With your permission, Google Analytics measures page visits and interactions such as call, text, email and kit-link clicks. The site’s analytics code does not send the contents of your contact-form fields. Google Analytics may use cookies and device/browser information. See how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Optional analytics stays off until you choose “Allow analytics.” “No thanks” leaves it off. You can change your choice using Cookie settings in the footer. Your choice is stored in this browser; if storage is unavailable, it may need to be chosen again. Withdrawing permission stops future analytics collection on this site but does not delete information already processed by Google.

Essential functions and external services

Your privacy preference and header-animation pause choice are stored locally. Security checks may use necessary browser storage. Calling, texting and emailing open your chosen apps. Kit purchases and downloads are handled by Gumroad, and website-care billing uses Stripe. Their own privacy policies and terms apply when you use those services. This website’s contact form does not collect payment-card information.

Records and privacy requests

Contact and service records may be retained as needed for follow-up, ongoing work, bookkeeping and resolving questions about services. To ask about your information, request a correction or deletion, or discuss a privacy concern, contact Josh. Some records may need to be kept for legal, accounting or dispute-resolution purposes; Josh will explain any limits that apply to your request.
