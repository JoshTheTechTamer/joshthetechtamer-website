What costs extra

New features, redesigns, integrations, and big content work are quoted separately at $50/hour.

  • Requested changes: $50/hour, with or without the Care Plan. This means new content, new pages, design changes, new services or prices, and new photos.
  • Website audits: $50/hour
  • Hosting and domain after year one without the Care Plan: $120/year (included with the Care Plan)

If you’re not sure whether something is included, just ask. I’ll tell you before any work starts.

Included repairs restore your existing site’s functionality. New features, content changes, and work caused by changes to third-party services are quoted separately. Third-party outages and account restrictions are outside my control, but I can help you figure out the next step.

Good to know

A few plain answers.

What happens to the deposit?

The deposit covers work already done, so it isn’t refundable once the build or edits have started. You can walk away any time before go-live and you won’t owe the remaining balance.

Who owns the site?

Josh owns the design and code until you’ve paid in full. Then you own your content and site, and you can have the domain transferred to you anytime.

Do I have to share my passwords?

No. You never share passwords or email access. Booking, Google, and Facebook connect through embed codes and invites you send or approve.

Where is my site hosted?

Josh registers your domain on his Cloudflare account and hosts your site on his GitHub Pages and Cloudflare accounts, the same way this site is hosted.
