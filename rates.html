Good questions

Before we get started.

Which consults are free?

Texting me about tech help or scam help is free. So is the first conversation about a startup, a logo or a website demo. Tell me what’s going on and I’ll help figure out the next step. Appointments, house calls and office visits are $50 an hour.

What areas do you serve?

I’m based in Muscatine and make house calls about 30 miles around town: Fruitland, Wilton, West Liberty, Durant, Columbus Junction and the small towns nearby, plus the whole Quad Cities on both sides of the river (I’m there a couple times a week). Trips farther out may have a small added gas charge on your invoice. See the towns I go to, and tell me where you are when you reach out.

Can you help if I’m not good with technology?

Yes. Ask whatever you need to ask. The goal is to get it working and get you comfortable using it.

How do estimates for bigger projects work?

Start with a free text, or a free first conversation for a startup, logo or website demo. We go over the scope, what you’ll get and the estimated work before you decide.

Are equipment and advertising costs included?

The $50 an hour is for my work. Equipment, paid software, printing or ad budget gets discussed separately for your project.

Can I get help with a new business?

Yes. Logo and brand work, ad setup, websites, and practical help with the tools and tasks involved in getting started.

How do I book?

Text or call 563-261-0200, or send the form on the contact page. We set the time together. Sending a request doesn’t reserve a time by itself.

What should I have ready for a visit?

Keep the device and its usual cables handy. If something needs a password, you can type it in yourself. When we set up the visit, tell me where you are and what’s going on.

How are travel and payment handled?

Tell me your location when you ask for help. Visits at your place have a one-hour minimum at $50 an hour. Ask me to confirm any travel charge and how you’d like to pay before the visit.

Do you have a monthly subscription?

Not for tech help. You book help when you need it at $50 an hour, or get an estimate for a bigger project. The only monthly plan is the standard $25/month Care Plan for websites I build. See the website package.

Not sure what to ask for?

You don’t need the technical name for anything. Just tell me what’s happening.
