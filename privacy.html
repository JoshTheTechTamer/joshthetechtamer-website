Privacy policy

Effective date: November 13, 2026.

This page explains how The Tech Tamer LLC (based in Muscatine, Iowa) handles information you share with us.

What we collect

When you reach out through this website’s forms or through Facebook or Instagram lead ads, we may collect your name, email, phone number, business name, and town.

This site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics for basic traffic numbers, which doesn't use cookies. It also uses the Meta Pixel, which sets a cookie and lets Meta know which pages you visit, so we can tell whether our Facebook and Instagram ads are working and show them to people nearby who may need help. The pixel also lets Meta know when you tap to call or text us, or when you send our request form. It doesn't share what you type into the form. You can turn off ad personalization in your Facebook settings or block cookies in your browser.

How we use it

We use that information only to contact you about the services you asked for.

Sharing

We never sell your information. We only share it with the tools we use to run the business: Google (email and calendar), Meta (ads and lead ads), Stripe (payments), and Cloudflare (hosting and analytics).

Questions or deletion

To ask a question or ask us to delete your information, text 563-261-0200 or email joshthetechtamer@gmail.com.
