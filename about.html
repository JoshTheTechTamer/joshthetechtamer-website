About Josh

Your local tech guy.
Minus the tech-speak.

I’m Josh Sargent—the person behind The Tech Tamer. I help people and small businesses across Eastern Iowa get more comfortable with their technology.

Howdy there!

When your tech won’t cooperate,
I’m the guy you call.

I’ve been helping people work through technology problems since I was a kid in the ’90s. What started as helping family and friends grew into The Tech Tamer: practical, personal help for the things you use every day.

I’m no plumber. When a pipe leaks, I call someone who knows what they’re doing. Technology is my version of that—I’m here to help when a device won’t cooperate or you don’t know where to start.

Sometimes that means getting a printer connected. Sometimes it means setting up a TV, making a phone less confusing, or helping a small business bring its branding and technology together.

Whatever the project, you’ll work directly with me. I’ll explain the plan in plain English and help you understand what comes next.
