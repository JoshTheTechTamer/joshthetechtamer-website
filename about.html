About Josh

I’m Josh, the guy behind the top hat.

I’m Josh Sargent. I run The Tech Tamer out of Muscatine and help people and small businesses around Eastern Iowa get along better with their technology.

Text JoshCall Josh

Howdy.

I’ve been helping people sort out technology since I was a kid in the ’90s. It started with family and friends. Eventually it turned into The Tech Tamer, which is practical, in-person help with the stuff you use every day.

I’m no plumber. When a pipe leaks, I call someone who knows what they’re doing. Technology is my version of that. When a device won’t cooperate or you don’t know where to start, that’s my cue.

Some days that means getting a printer connected. Other days it’s setting up a TV, making a phone less confusing, or helping a small business get its logo, website and computers on the same page.

Whatever it is, you work with me directly. I’ll explain the plan without the tech-speak and make sure you know what comes next.
